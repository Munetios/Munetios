# Munetios Beta availability

## Supported locations

The Munetios Beta currently supports the United States, Brazil, European Union member countries, the Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, India, Argentina, and Nigeria.

## Locations not yet supported

Australia, the United Kingdom, New Zealand, and locations not listed above are not yet supported. Availability can expand after legal, privacy, security, localization, and operational reviews are complete.

## Country selection

Country selectors show supported Beta locations only. Automatic country detection can help select regional defaults without displaying an IP address in the interface.
