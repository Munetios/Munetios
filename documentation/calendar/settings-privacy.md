# Calendar settings, imports, and privacy

## Appearance and time settings

Open Calendar settings to choose the locale, time zone, week layout, working hours, and visible calendar options. Country choices are limited to locations where the Munetios Beta is supported.

## Notifications and permissions

Calendar notifications require browser permission. If notifications do not appear, review the site permission in your browser and the notification setting inside Calendar. Munetios does not bypass browser or operating-system permission controls.

## Import and export

Use the import controls for supported calendar files and providers. Review imported events before deleting the original source. Exported files may contain private event details, so store and share them carefully.

## Encrypted synchronization

Signed-in Calendar data uses the account-backed encrypted calendar vault. Guest or temporary data may remain only on the current device. Keep the page open until a save completes before closing the browser or switching devices.
