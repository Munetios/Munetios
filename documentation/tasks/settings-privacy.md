# Tasks settings, synchronization, and privacy

## Account and device behavior

Confirm that the correct account is selected before troubleshooting missing tasks. Signed-in experiences can synchronize supported task data; guest and temporary work may remain on the current device.

## Education controls

Managed student accounts have restricted settings and protected teacher-assigned work. Teachers can manage students and their available controls from Account settings. Munetios Classroom is coming soon.

## Troubleshoot synchronization

Keep the page open until a task save finishes. If a task does not appear on another device, verify the selected account and list, refresh once, and check the archived or trash views. Avoid submitting the same edit repeatedly while progress is visible.

## Privacy and deletion

Do not put passwords, recovery codes, private keys, or unnecessary sensitive information in a task. Review collaborators before sharing. Use Trash for recoverable removal and read any confirmation carefully before permanently deleting data.
