# Get started with Munetios Meet

![Munetios Meet logo](/meet.png)

Munetios Meet provides browser-based video meetings with device controls, meeting history, activities, and privacy settings.

![Meet overview on localhost](/documentation/screenshots/meet-overview.png)

## Start or join a meeting

Choose **New meeting** to create a room, or enter a valid meeting code or link to join. Review your name and device state before entering. Share a meeting link only with intended participants.

## Prepare your devices

Select the microphone, camera, and speaker you want to use. Browser permission is required before Meet can access a microphone or camera. If a device is missing, reconnect it and reopen the browser’s site permissions.

## In-meeting controls

Use the meeting bar to mute audio, turn the camera on or off, present supported content, open activities, and leave. Controls expose accessible names and remain keyboard operable.
