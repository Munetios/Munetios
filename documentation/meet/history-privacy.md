# Meet history, settings, and privacy

## Meeting history

The history page lists supported recent meeting activity. Use it to revisit meeting details or identify a previous call. Deleting a local history item does not delete records that another participant or organization is required to retain.

## Blocked people and safety

Use the available safety controls to block unwanted contact and leave a meeting immediately when needed. Report abuse without including unrelated private information.

## Settings storage

Signed-in Meet settings are saved through the account Meet settings service. Guest settings use browser storage on the current device. Clearing browser data can remove guest preferences.

## Privacy checklist

- Check the camera background before joining.
- Confirm the selected microphone and speaker.
- Share only the intended tab or window.
- Get consent before recording.
- Leave and report unexpected or unsafe meetings.
