# Meeting controls, activities, and recordings

## Participants and chat

Open the participant panel to review who is present. Use meeting chat for information appropriate for everyone in the room. The host or meeting policy may limit actions for some participants.

## Present content

Screen presentation uses the browser’s sharing picker. Confirm the exact tab, window, or screen before sharing. Stop presenting as soon as the shared content is no longer needed.

## Activities

Meet includes supported collaborative activities such as chess and word games. Activity availability can depend on the meeting state, account, and device size.

## Recording

Recording uses supported browser media technology and the selected recording quality. Obtain consent from every participant and follow local law before recording. If recording is unavailable, verify browser capabilities, permissions, storage, and account settings.
